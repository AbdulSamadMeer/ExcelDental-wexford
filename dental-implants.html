Tooth Replacement

Replace Missing Teeth for Good

A dental implant is a small titanium post placed in your jawbone that acts as a permanent artificial root. Once integrated with the bone, it supports a lifelike porcelain crown — giving you a replacement tooth that looks, feels, and functions exactly like a natural one.

Unlike dentures or bridges, implants preserve jawbone density, don't rely on adjacent teeth for support, and can last a lifetime with proper care. If you're missing one tooth, several, or a full arch, we have an implant solution to restore your smile and your confidence. We use advanced 3D CT imaging and custom surgical guides to map out your treatment before we begin, so every step is carefully planned for you. That means a safer, gentler procedure, greater comfort during healing, and results you can feel confident about for years to come.

Implant options available

  • Single tooth implant with porcelain crown
  • Multiple implants for several missing teeth
  • Implant-supported fixed bridge (no removable parts)
  • All-on-4® full arch restoration
  • Implant-supported dentures (snap-on overdentures)
  • Bone grafting to build up insufficient bone volume
  • Sinus lift procedures when needed

The Implant Process

A step-by-step journey to a permanent, beautiful smile.

1

Consultation & 3D Scan

We evaluate your bone density, gum health, and overall oral health to confirm you're a candidate.

2

Implant Placement

The titanium post is placed into the jawbone under local anaesthetic — most patients report minimal discomfort.

3

Healing (Osseointegration)

Over 3–6 months, the implant fuses with your bone, creating a rock-solid foundation.

4

Crown Placement

Your custom porcelain crown is attached — blending seamlessly with your natural teeth.
