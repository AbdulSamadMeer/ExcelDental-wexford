Tooth Replacement

Eat, Speak & Smile with Confidence Again

Modern dentures are nothing like the ill-fitting, unnatural appliances of the past. At Excel Dental of Wexford, we craft custom dentures using high-quality materials and precise measurements to ensure a comfortable, secure fit that complements your facial features and looks completely natural.

Whether you've lost a few teeth or are missing a full arch, we offer the right denture solution for your unique situation — including implant-supported options that provide dramatically improved stability and comfort over traditional dentures.

Denture types we provide

  • Complete dentures (full upper and/or lower arch)
  • Partial dentures (when some natural teeth remain)
  • Immediate dentures (placed the same day as extractions)
  • Implant-supported overdentures (snap-on for ultimate stability)
  • Implant-supported fixed bridges (non-removable)
  • Denture relines, repairs, and adjustments

Your Denture Journey

From impressions to a perfectly fitting, natural-looking smile.

1

Initial Consultation

We assess your oral health, discuss your options, and explain what to expect at each stage.

2

Impressions & Records

Detailed impressions and bite measurements are taken to create a denture unique to your mouth.

3

Try-In Appointment

A wax try-in lets you and the dentist evaluate the fit, appearance, and bite before finalisation.

4

Delivery & Adjustment

Your finished dentures are placed, and we schedule follow-up visits to fine-tune the fit.
