Surgical Care

Precision Care When It Matters Most

When a dental problem requires a surgical solution, you want a team you can trust completely. Our dentists perform a full range of oral surgery procedures with skill and care, in a calm, professional setting designed to put you at ease from the moment you arrive.

We use local anaesthesia and, when appropriate, sedation options to ensure you're comfortable throughout every procedure. Detailed pre- and post-operative instructions mean your recovery is smooth and well-supported.

Procedures we perform

  • Simple and surgical tooth extractions
  • Wisdom tooth removal
  • Bone grafting (socket preservation)
  • Sinus lift augmentation
  • Soft-tissue (gum) procedures
  • Pre-prosthetic surgery (preparing for dentures or implants)

What to Expect

A clear overview of your surgical visit, from consultation to recovery.

1

Consultation & X-Rays

We examine the area, review imaging, and discuss your surgical options, anaesthesia, and recovery timeline.

2

Pre-Op Preparation

We provide written pre-operative instructions covering diet, medications, and what to arrange for your recovery.

3

The Procedure

With you fully comfortable using local anaesthesia, the procedure is performed efficiently and carefully.

4

Recovery & Follow-Up

We provide post-op care instructions and schedule a follow-up to ensure healing is on track.
