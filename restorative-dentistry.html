Restorative Dentistry

Bringing Your Smile Back to Full Strength

Life happens — teeth crack, cavities form, old restorations wear out. Excel Dental of Wexford's restorative services are designed to repair damage quickly and effectively using the highest-quality, tooth-matched materials so your smile looks and feels completely natural.

Whether you need a simple filling or a comprehensive full-mouth reconstruction, our team develops a treatment plan around your specific needs, priorities, and schedule. We are committed to preserving as much natural tooth structure as possible with every restoration.

Restorative treatments we offer

  • Tooth-coloured composite fillings
  • Porcelain and zirconia dental crowns
  • Tooth-supported fixed bridges
  • Inlays and onlays (partial crowns)
  • Full-mouth reconstruction
  • Emergency same-day restorations
  • Replacement of failing metal restorations

What to Expect

A clear, comfortable path from damaged tooth to full restoration.

1

Assessment

We examine and X-ray the affected area to fully understand the extent of damage or decay.

2

Treatment Plan

We review your options, costs, and timelines — and prioritise urgent needs versus elective improvements.

3

Restoration

Using local anaesthetic for comfort, we precisely remove decay or shape the tooth and place the restoration.

4

Final Check

We check your bite, polish the restoration, and ensure you're comfortable before you leave.
