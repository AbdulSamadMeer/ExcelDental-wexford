Dental Sleep Medicine

Comfortable, non-invasive oral appliance therapy for sleep-related breathing disorders.

Support for better sleep

Dental sleep medicine focuses on treating sleep-related breathing disorders, including snoring and obstructive sleep apnea (OSA). These conditions can affect overall health, energy, and quality of life.

Dr. Dhileep Jinna is a Diplomate of the American Board of Dental Sleep Medicine and is specially trained in treating sleep-disordered breathing with oral appliance therapy.

What is sleep apnea?

Sleep apnea is a condition in which breathing repeatedly stops and starts during sleep. The most common type, obstructive sleep apnea, occurs when the airway becomes blocked.

Common symptoms include

  • Loud snoring
  • Daytime fatigue or sleepiness
  • Morning headaches
  • Difficulty concentrating
  • Dry mouth or sore throat upon waking
  • Restless or poor-quality sleep

Oral appliance therapy

Custom-fitted oral appliances are similar to a mouthguard and are worn during sleep. They gently reposition the jaw to help keep the airway open, improve airflow, and may reduce snoring.

These devices are quiet and portable, and may be an option for some patients who cannot tolerate CPAP or prefer a non-invasive approach. A dentist and physician or sleep specialist can help determine appropriate care.

Our process

  1. Consultation: Review symptoms and medical history.
  2. Collaboration: Coordinate with your physician or sleep specialist.
  3. Custom appliance: Take digital impressions and fit a personalized appliance.
  4. Follow-up care: Make adjustments to support comfort and effectiveness.
