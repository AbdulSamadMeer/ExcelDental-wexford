Family Dentistry

A Welcoming Place for Young Smiles

We warmly welcome children of all ages at Excel Dental of Wexford. While we're not a dedicated pediatric specialist office, our entire team is experienced, patient, and passionate about making every child's visit comfortable, positive, and even fun.

Early dental care is essential. Establishing healthy habits and a positive relationship with the dentist sets the foundation for a lifetime of strong, healthy teeth. We work at every child's pace, explaining each step in kid-friendly language, so they leave feeling proud — not scared.

Children's services include

  • First dental visit (recommended by age 1 or first tooth)
  • Routine cleanings and check-ups
  • Fluoride treatments for cavity prevention
  • Dental sealants (protective coating on molars)
  • Digital X-rays (low-dose, child-safe)
  • Sports mouthguards
  • Cavity fillings with tooth-coloured composite
  • Orthodontic screening and early intervention referrals

What to Expect at Your Child's Visit

A step-by-step look at how we make every visit a positive one.

1

Warm Welcome

We greet your child by name and take time to make them feel safe and comfortable before any treatment begins.

2

Gentle Cleaning

The hygienist cleans teeth slowly and gently, explaining each tool with fun "dentist language" kids enjoy.

3

Doctor Check-Up

The dentist counts, checks, and examines all teeth, noting development milestones and any areas of concern.

4

Home Tips & Next Visit

We send kids home with brushing tips, a goody bag, and the next appointment ready to go.
