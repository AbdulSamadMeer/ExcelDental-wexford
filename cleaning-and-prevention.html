Preventive Dentistry

The Foundation of a Healthy Smile

Regular dental checkups and professional cleanings are the single most important thing you can do for your oral health. Even with excellent brushing and flossing, plaque builds up in areas that are impossible to reach at home — only a professional cleaning can fully remove it.

At Excel Dental of Wexford, every cleaning visit includes a comprehensive examination so we can catch small issues early, before they become large — and expensive — problems. We combine state-of-the-art digital X-rays with a thorough visual inspection to give you a complete picture of your oral health.

What's included in your visit

  • Full oral examination by one of our dentists
  • Professional plaque and tartar removal (scaling)
  • Tooth polishing to remove surface stains
  • Oral cancer screening
  • Gum health assessment (periodontal charting)
  • Digital X-rays (as recommended)
  • Fluoride treatment (optional)
  • Personalised home-care tips and product recommendations

What to Expect

A typical cleaning visit is comfortable, quick, and leaves your mouth feeling great.

1

Check-In & X-Rays

We update your records and take any needed digital X-rays to check for hidden concerns.

2

Hygienist Cleaning

Your hygienist removes plaque and tartar buildup, then polishes teeth to a smooth, clean finish.

3

Doctor Exam

The dentist reviews X-rays, checks for cavities, gum health, and any other concerns.

4

Home-Care Review

We discuss your personalised brushing and flossing routine and schedule your next visit.
