Cosmetic Dentistry

A Smile You'll Love to Share

Cosmetic dentistry goes beyond function — it's about the way your smile makes you feel. Whether you want to brighten discoloured teeth, repair a chip, close gaps, or completely transform your appearance, Excel Dental of Wexford offers a full menu of cosmetic options tailored to your goals and budget.

We start with a detailed consultation to understand what you want to achieve, then create a personalised treatment plan using the most advanced, minimally invasive techniques available. The result: a smile that looks completely natural and uniquely yours.

Cosmetic treatments we offer

  • Professional in-office teeth whitening
  • Take-home whitening trays (custom fitted)
  • Porcelain veneers (ultra-thin, lifelike shells)
  • Composite bonding for chips and gaps
  • Tooth-coloured fillings (replace old metal fillings)
  • Smile makeovers (combined treatment planning)
  • Gum contouring for a balanced gumline
