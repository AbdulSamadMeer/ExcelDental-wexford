Dr. Dhileep Jinna

Dr. Jinna graduated from the Boston University Goldman School of Dental Medicine and practices general and cosmetic dentistry in McCandless and Wexford, Pennsylvania. Before dental school, he earned an MBA in Healthcare Management from the University of Findlay.

He treats patients of all ages, with a special interest in preventive and restorative dentistry, dental implants, and dental sleep medicine. Dr. Jinna is a Diplomate of the American Board of Dental Sleep Medicine and serves in the United States Army Reserves.

Committed to quality, integrity, and service, he strives to accommodate patients experiencing dental emergencies or toothaches with same-day appointments whenever possible.
